## Monthly Workflow

**Week 1** — Pick the topic, jot down the angle and any personal experience to tie in.  
**Week 2** — Write the full draft. Aim for 1200–2000 words.  
**Week 3** — Read it aloud. Cut anything that doesn't add value. Add one concrete example or diagram if missing.  
**Week 4** — Publish. Write a 3–5 sentence LinkedIn summary with a hook and link. Post on Tuesday or Wednesday morning for best reach.

Target publish day: **first Tuesday of each month**.

---

## How to Keep Generating Topics

Good post ideas come from one of four places:

1. **Something you just built or learned** — the PPO post is the model here. If you're doing it anyway, write about it.
2. **A concept you had to explain to someone** — if you caught yourself explaining something to a classmate or colleague, it's a post.
3. **Something that confused you until it clicked** — the moment of clarity is the post. "I thought X but it's actually Y" is a great structure.
4. **A tool or technique you wish you'd known earlier** — practitioner advice beats textbook summaries every time.
